# BugReplay — SaaS revision voice script

Target: 92.5 seconds, six independently generated English voice blocks. Natural, confident delivery with short pauses; approximately 145–155 words per minute, without a presenter voice. Timings below are scene windows, not instructions to stretch speech. Measure the supplied audio before assembly.

## 1. Hook — 0–8 seconds

“The cart disappeared.” BugReplay turns browser symptoms into evidence you can actually run.

## 2. Investigate — 8–24 seconds

Give it a URL and the expected behavior. The model explores the interface, learns from browser observations, and records the complete setup. No source code. No hidden trigger recipe. This investigation found the disappearing cart.

## 3. Verify — 24–38.8 seconds

Then the model steps aside. The saved Playwright test runs twice in clean browsers. Both fail on the intended cart preservation assertion. Two items become zero. That is the proof.

## 4. Control — 38.8–56.5 seconds

Change only the base URL. The exact same test passes on the corrected shop. Same steps. Same assertion. You get the test, report, screenshots, and original recordings.

## 5. Improve and account — 56.5–76.5 seconds

Neatlogs exposed malformed responses that interrupted exploration. We added bounded recovery, then traced the real rerun. Entire links the fix to its checkpoint and diff. CFO AI models pricing and separates measured usage from estimates. Failed attempts count too.

## 6. Limits and close — 76.5–92.5 seconds

One controlled shop. One defect class. One of three final buggy investigations confirmed it. Complete cash billing remains unknown. BugReplay. From a vague report to a verified reproduction.
