# Visual direction

A clear engineering handoff: the complaint leads to the actual interface, the real replay, and precise evidence, with the boundary between tested behavior and remaining work always visible.

Canvas: 1280×720. Base #edf2f7; foreground #10283e; accent #2453c9; supporting text #405a70; paper #ffffff; evidence green #24664c and regression red #963f45. One consistent background, restrained rule lines, and generous text spacing.

Use HyperFrames' built-in Inter, the product UI's familiar sans-serif register, in 400 and 700 weights. Headlines 44–60px, body 24–30px, persistent qualification 18px. Small source labels are 16px; they are supporting attribution, never the main message. Readable real screenshot crops are evidence, not reconstructed UI.

Header: product name on the left and the persistent qualification on the right. Scene content is left aligned. Footer identifies this as a product preview and names HyperFrames. Screenshots and the actual replay are the focal objects; numerical results are paired with the exact kind of assertion they measure.

No decorative animation or fabricated interactions. Editorial hard cuts are intentional. Still images hold long enough to read; the three-second replay plays once at its original speed. Each composition has one paused timeline for deterministic seeking.
